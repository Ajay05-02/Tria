flowstate

Method

Educational prototype. Not a medical device or a substitute for coronary angiography. Trained on 303 patients from one centre in Tehran.

flowstate predicts coronary artery disease from routine tests and shows which artery is at risk.

  1. Pick or edit a patient in Table 1. The prediction updates as you type.
  2. Read the risk on the heart and in panel B. Click an artery to focus on it.
  3. See why in panel C. Click a factor to jump to that value.
